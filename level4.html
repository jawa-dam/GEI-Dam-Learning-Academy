What does “operations” mean?

Operations are the actions used to control a working system. In this Academy model, that means opening or closing a gate, watching flow, and releasing water in a controlled way.

Learning note: The Academy uses an engineering analogy to explore Genesis language. These activities are designed to test connections and vocabulary, not to claim that every term has only one meaning.
